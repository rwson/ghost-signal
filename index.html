How detection works

Values and successive changes are compared to their median using median absolute deviation (MAD). Missing intervals are inferred from the median positive time interval and may reflect scheduled gaps. Zero MAD uses a conservative fallback. Sensitivity changes the deviation threshold; duplicate timestamps are excluded from interval estimation. These heuristics do not establish causation.
